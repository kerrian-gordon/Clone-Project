John is a boy with a dog named Rover. That dog loves to play fetch with red balls. One day they were on a walk. While on that walk, they saw Sarah and her dog Charlie playing fetch with a red ball. Rover was excited. He ran over and caught the ball before Charlie could get it. John scolded Rover and told him he was a bad dog. John picked up the ball, gave it back to Sarah, and apologized. Then John found another red ball nearby and played fetch with Rover for a little while. Rover was a happy dog that day.
